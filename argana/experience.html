01 · Arrival

From the south of Morocco.

Afro House and Afrotech, played deep and slow-burning. A Moroccan DJ based in Barcelona.

02 · Rooted

Roots first.

The argan tree grows almost nowhere but southern Morocco. It survives the drought by reaching deep, drops its leaves in the dry season, and comes back green. ARGANA plays the same way: patient, rooted, hypnotic.

03 · Two shores

Between two shores.

Raised between Agadir and Casablanca. Built in Barcelona. European club culture and North African heritage in one set, without the costume.

04 · The ritual

He built his own room.

Barcelona had no true home for Afro House, so ARGANA founded ZAYA and plays it as resident. Two editions at Hotel El Palace.
