Biography

Deep roots, slow fire.

Two nights at Hotel El Palace. Both sold out. Not one paid ad.

That is how ARGANA introduced himself to Barcelona. With roots in Agadir and Casablanca and a home in Barcelona, he built ZAYA, an invitation-only Afro House ritual, because the city had no real home for the music he wanted to play. He curates it, films it and plays it as resident.

Behind the decks he works the deep end of Afro House and Afrotech: long percussive builds, three-step rhythms, and an Arabic phrase only when the room is ready for it.

He also shoots and cuts his own films, so every night he plays has a life after it. His first original productions are next, built from the same roots.

Why ARGANA. Named after the argan tree of southern Morocco, which stays alive through the dry season with roots that reach deep. Roots, resilience, return.

Short bio · for flyers & listings

ARGANA is a Barcelona-based Afro House and Afrotech DJ with roots in Agadir and Casablanca. He founded ZAYA, an invitation-only Afro House night that sold out both of its editions at Hotel El Palace without a single paid ad, and plays it as resident.
