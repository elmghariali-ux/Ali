Who is ARGANA?

ARGANA is a Moroccan DJ based in Barcelona, with roots in Agadir and Casablanca. His sets sit at the deeper end of Afro House and Afrotech: rolling percussion, long tension, and grooves that build patiently instead of chasing the drop. Moroccan rhythms and Arabic vocal phrases appear where they belong, never as a gimmick.

The name comes from the argan tree of southern Morocco, which survives the dry season with roots that reach deep. See the full press kit, or step into The Root System, an immersive version of his story.

Afro House and Afrotech, in short

What is Afro House?

A style of house music built on African rhythms, percussion and vocals. It grew out of South Africa and is now played in clubs worldwide, usually between 118 and 125 BPM.

What is Afrotech?

Afrotech keeps the African groove but uses darker, more electronic and hypnotic sound design, closer to techno. ARGANA moves between both, with three-step rhythms in the mix.

Where can I hear Afro House in Barcelona?

At ZAYA, ARGANA's invitation-only ritual. Follow @arganamusic for his other dates.
