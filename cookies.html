Cookies

Last updated · 20 October 2026

This website does not use cookies. That is why you never see a cookie banner here.

No cookies, no tracking

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, social media plugins or any third-party cookies, and we do not store anything on your device.

How we count visits

We use Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies. It records anonymous, aggregated information — the page visited, the referring site, country, device type and browser — and recognises a returning visit only through a temporary anonymous code that is discarded after 24 hours. It cannot identify you.

What loads from where

The fonts, images, films and music on this site are all served from zayaritual.com itself. Other services are contacted only when you act: Instagram when you follow a link to it, and our form provider when you send an invitation request (see the privacy policy).

If this changes

If we ever use cookies that need your consent, we will ask for it first and update this page.
